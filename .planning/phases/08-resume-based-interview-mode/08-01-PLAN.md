<objective>
Implement Resume-Based Interview Mode where candidates upload their resume (PDF / DOCX) to R2 storage, the system parses candidate skills, past roles, projects, and work experience, and dynamically synthesizes candidate-specific interview questions grounded in their actual background.
</objective>

<execution_context>
- Candidate selects `Resume Based Interview` on `InterviewSetupPage.jsx`.
- Drag & Drop file uploader uploads resume to R2 storage / local storage.
- Backend extracts text and calls `nlp-service` `/generate-resume-questions` to synthesize candidate-tailored questions.
- Session persists `resumeUrl` and `resumeText` in MongoDB.
</execution_context>
