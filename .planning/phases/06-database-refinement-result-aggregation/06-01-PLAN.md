<objective>
Refine the Database Result Aggregation Engine in `reportController.js` and `analysisService.js` to compile per-answer Face, Voice, NLP, and Writing metrics into a cohesive, persisted `Session.reportData` object with consolidated candidate strengths and areas of improvement.
</objective>

<execution_context>
- Transcribed and evaluated answers are stored on `Session.answers`.
- Aggregate scores across Face, Voice, NLP, and Writing dimensions into dynamic non-hardcoded percentages.
- Produce consolidated `strengths` and `improvements` lists.
- Display in `ReportPage.jsx` with light theme visual metrics, per-question accordions, and report summary downloads.
</execution_context>
